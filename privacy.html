Privacy
Policy.

How SMUVPAY collects, uses and protects your information. Last updated 20 November 2026.

Who we are

SMUVPAY ("we", "us") provides payment, financial infrastructure and capital advisory services. For data-protection purposes, the SMUVPAY entity you deal with is the controller of your personal data.

Information we collect

We collect only what we need to respond to you and provide our services:

  • Enquiry data — name, email, company and the message you submit through our enquiry form.
  • Communication data — correspondence you send to us by email or otherwise.
  • Usage data — basic, aggregate technical data such as pages visited, device type and approximate location, where analytics are in use.

How we use it

  • To respond to enquiries and start a conversation about our services.
  • To carry out onboarding and due diligence where you proceed with us.
  • To comply with legal, regulatory and anti-money-laundering obligations.
  • To improve and secure the website.

Legal basis

We process your data on the basis of your consent, the performance of a contract or steps before entering one, our legitimate interests in operating our business, and compliance with legal obligations.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us operate (hosting, email, analytics) and, where necessary, with regulated partners, professional advisers or authorities as required by law. We retain data only for as long as needed for the purpose and any applicable retention period.

Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data, and to object to processing. To exercise any of these, contact support@smuvpay.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@smuvpay.com. Registered addresses are in our site footer.
